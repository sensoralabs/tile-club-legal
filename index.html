Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Sensora Labs ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile application Tile Club: Match & Mind Games (tile_club.app). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you play our game.

1. No Account Registration Required

Tile Club does not require users to create an account, log in, or submit personal profile details such as your name, home address, or phone number to play the game. Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device.

2. Data Collected & Shared via Third-Party SDKs

To keep the game free to play, monitor stability, and prevent invalid ad traffic, our app integrates trusted third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Services). In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, the following data types may be collected and shared automatically:

  • Device or Other IDs: Google Advertising ID (AAID), app set ID, and device identifiers used for serving advertisements, frequency capping, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse, IP-derived country/region location used by advertising partners to serve regionally relevant ads and comply with local regulations. Precise GPS location is never collected.
  • App Activity & Diagnostics: Basic ad interaction metrics (ad impressions/clicks) and crash/performance diagnostics to maintain app stability.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

  • Advertising & Marketing: Displaying banner, interstitial, or rewarded video ads via Google AdMob.
  • Analytics & App Performance: Understanding gameplay stability and diagnosing technical crashes.
  • Fraud Prevention, Security & Compliance: Detecting invalid ad clicks, automated bots, and ensuring compliance with applicable laws (including GDPR/EEA consent rules).

4. Data Security & Encryption

All data transmitted off your device by integrated third-party SDKs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. You can reset or limit your Advertising ID anytime in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Third-party advertising partners process data under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy & Terms.

6. Children's Privacy

Tile Club is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

7. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling practices, contact us at sensoralabs@gmail.com.
